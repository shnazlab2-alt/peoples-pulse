## Timeless Question
When does the hunger for a single, clean answer begin to shrink the room where a truer, more living question could still grow?

## Venn Domains
- Domain A: Resolute Clarity (the practiced drive toward unambiguous conclusions, decisive maps, and the relief of “now I know”)
- Domain B: Generative Ambiguity (the capacity to remain inside the unfinished, the partial, the question that keeps changing shape as you live it)

**Overlap:** The quiet threshold where the desire for resolution meets the recognition that some truths only reveal themselves while the question is still open.

**Color of the Overlap:** Warm Ochre  
**Mindset of the Overlap:** Patient Discernment — the discipline of noticing when the urge to close a question is serving the question’s depth, and when it is merely protecting us from having to stay with it longer.
