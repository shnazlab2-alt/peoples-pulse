## Timeless Question
When does the refusal to reduce a complexity begin to leave the people who must still act without a map they can carry?

## Venn Domains
- Domain A: Unreduced Complexity (the practiced refusal to smooth away the joints, the exceptions, or the residual tensions that would make the account false)
- Domain B: Carryable Map (the recognition that a description too dense to be held, remembered, or shared under ordinary conditions leaves the people who already must move unable to orient, coordinate, or correct)

**Overlap:** The living hinge where fidelity and portability become two conditions of the same orientation — a complexity that stays true because it has not treated further density as if it cost the carriers nothing.

**Color of the Overlap:** River Stone  
**Mindset of the Overlap:** Portable Fidelity — the practiced capacity to keep the joints visible without pretending the people who must still travel can carry the entire unreduced field in their hands.
