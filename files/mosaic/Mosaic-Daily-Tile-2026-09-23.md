# Mosaic Daily Tile — 2026-09-23

## Timeless Question
When does the courage to name what is wrong begin to crowd out the patience required to hear what is still unfinished in the other person?

## Venn Domains
- Domain A: Moral Speech (the duty to name harm, refuse euphemism, and place a true word in the room before the room can pretend it did not hear)
- Domain B: Hospitable Listening (the discipline of receiving another’s account, including the parts that do not yet fit your map, before you decide what the moment means)

**Overlap:** The thin place where speaking and hearing are two faces of the same respect — a word offered without having first closed the ear.

**Color of the Overlap:** Quiet Sage  
**Mindset of the Overlap:** Dialogic Courage — the willingness to risk being revised by what you hear even while you still owe the room a sentence that is not a flinch.
