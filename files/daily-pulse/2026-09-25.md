The citizen wants the Friday ballot counted as if Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday were rest. The clerk can stamp the ballot and let the remembered instrument speak for the missing nights, or she can leave the ballot uncounted until someone says what those nights were. Who answers for a choice made with a tool that remembers, over days that left no memory of their own?
